00 / Mission briefing

Before the mission

The operator wears the Quest headset and reads the clues on the workbench. The expert reads this manual on another screen or on paper and gives instructions by voice. No connected expert app is required. For the intended challenge, the expert should not watch the operator's headset view.

Choose and start

Preset settings; these can be changed before arming.
PresetTimeModules
TRAIN / 26 minutes2
STANDARD / 35 minutes3
EXPERT / 33 minutes3
  1. Select a preset to open its briefing. Choosing a preset applies its default time and module count.
  2. For a custom mission, use the MISSION TIMER and MODULE COUNT minus/plus controls in the lobby or briefing. Time ranges from 30 seconds to 20 minutes in 30-second steps. Module count ranges from 1 to 12. Changing these settings prepares a CUSTOM briefing.
  3. When both players are ready, press the selected green BEGIN TRAIN, BEGIN STANDARD, BEGIN EXPERT, or BEGIN CUSTOM control. A three-second arming countdown runs before the clues appear and the mission timer starts.
  4. Read the serial number between the timer and strike readout. Rules use its final digit. Even digits are 0, 2, 4, 6, 8; odd digits are 1, 3, 5, 7, 9.

Operate the console

Point at a control and press the index-finger trigger. Either controller works. Release the trigger fully between presses. Middle-finger grip is not the puzzle-button input. Select the toggle controls to change them; no physical grabbing or cutting tool is needed.

Only three modules are visible per page. With more than three modules, use < PAGE and PAGE > at the rear corners of the console. Pages wrap around. Progress and switch positions are preserved, and the clock keeps running. Any module can be solved first.

Suggested callout: “Module 4, Wire Bank. Serial ends in 8. Wires one to four are CYAN, WHITE, AMBER, VIOLET.”

Each group of five generated modules contains all five types once. Types can repeat on larger missions, with fresh clues. Always include the printed module number in callouts.

Three strikes or zero time ends the mission. The countdown does not pause for page changes, the Meta menu, or removing the headset. A solved module shows SECURED and ignores further inputs.

01 / Dial, emblem and four numbered buttons

Direction Relay

Identify: a metal dial with an engraved emblem, a printed emblem name and START number, four numbered buttons, and three progress lamps.

Ask for: emblem (SUN, MOON or STAR), START number, and final serial digit.

EmblemUse this number order
SUNForward: 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 1
MOONBackward: 4 → 3 → 2 → 1 → 4
STAREven final serial digit: forward.
Odd final serial digit: backward.

Press the printed START number, then the next two numbers in the chosen order. This is exactly three presses. Use the button numbers; the dial itself does not turn or accept input.

Example: MOON / START 1 → press 1, 4, 3.
Example: STAR / START 4 / serial ending 0 → press 4, 1, 2.

Correct intermediate presses show ACCEPTED and light the progress indicators.

Wrong press: one strike, and the entire sequence resets. The wrong press is not reused as a new starting press. Begin again with the printed START number. The emblem and START number do not change.

02 / Four colored cables and CUT controls

Wire Bank

Identify: four curved cables with terminal sockets, numbered color tags, and CUT controls. The possible colors are CYAN, AMBER, WHITE and VIOLET.

Ask for: all four colors by wire number, and final serial digit.

Apply the first matching rule below:

  1. If there is exactly one WHITE wire, cut that wire. This takes priority over the serial rules.
  2. Otherwise, if the final serial digit is even, cut the lowest-numbered AMBER wire. If there are no AMBER wires, cut wire 2.
  3. Otherwise, the final serial digit is odd: cut the highest-numbered CYAN wire. If there are no CYAN wires, cut wire 3.

Select the matching CUT number with the index trigger. The numbered wire row is also selectable. A single correct selection secures the module and visibly separates that cable. Do not cut every wire.

Example: WHITE, AMBER, CYAN, WHITE; serial ends in 8 → there are two WHITE wires, so use the even rule and cut 2.
Example: VIOLET, CYAN, AMBER, CYAN; serial ends in 7 → cut 4, the highest-numbered CYAN wire.

Wrong cut: one strike. The module remains available with the same colors and target. Recheck the rules and try the correct wire.

03 / Three toggle levers and CHECK POWER

Power Array

Identify: a CHANNEL number, three metal toggle levers labeled A, B and C, ON/OFF readouts, and a CHECK POWER button.

Ask for: CHANNEL number (1–8) and final serial digit.

SwitchSet ON when…Otherwise
AThe CHANNEL number is even.OFF
BThe CHANNEL number is 5, 6, 7 or 8.OFF
CThe final serial digit is even.OFF

All three switches begin OFF. Select a switch to toggle it. The lever position, color and printed ON/OFF readout update together. Once all three settings match the table, press CHECK POWER.

Example: CHANNEL 6 / serial ending 7 → A ON, B ON, C OFF, then CHECK POWER.
Example: CHANNEL 3 / serial ending 0 → A OFF, B OFF, C ON, then CHECK POWER.

Incorrect CHECK POWER: one strike. Switch positions are preserved; adjust them before checking again. Toggling switches by itself never adds a strike.

04 / Engraved keys and ALPHA/BETA plate

Symbol Order

Identify: a plate marked ALPHA or BETA, three engraved symbol keys with word labels, and three progress lamps. Available symbols are SUN, MOON, STAR, WAVE, LEAF and RING.

Ask for: plate name and the three displayed symbol names.

ALPHA priority — read left to right

RING → LEAF → SUN → WAVE → MOON → STAR

BETA priority — read left to right

STAR → MOON → WAVE → SUN → LEAF → RING

Use the matching priority list. Skip every symbol that is not displayed. Press the three displayed symbols in the resulting order, regardless of where their keys sit on the panel. Each key is used exactly once.

Example: ALPHA with STAR, SUN and RING → press RING, SUN, STAR.
Example: BETA with LEAF, WAVE and MOON → press MOON, WAVE, LEAF.

Wrong symbol: one strike, and the whole sequence resets. The plate and symbols stay the same. Press all three again in the correct order; do not resume from the last correct key.

05 / Flashing optical lens and badge

Pulse Lock

Identify: a circular lens with metal bezel and cooling fins, a badge marked AZURE or LIME, and four numbered buttons.

Ask for: badge name, and flashes in one complete group.

Count the central lens flashes. Each group has 1–4 flashes, followed by a longer pause, and repeats. If observation starts halfway through a group, wait for the long pause and count the next complete group.

Flashes per groupAZURE: pressLIME: press
142
214
331
423

Press the single number from the table. The number is not necessarily the flash count. The badge's written name selects the column; room lighting and the lamp's color do not.

Example: LIME / three flashes → press 1.
Example: AZURE / one flash → press 4.

Wrong number: one strike. The badge and flash count stay the same. Observe another group if needed, then try again.

Lobby and replay

LOBBY returns immediately from briefing or results. During the arming countdown or a running mission, press LOBBY, then CONFIRM LOBBY within three seconds to abandon the mission. A running timer continues until confirmed. If confirmation expires, begin the two-press confirmation again.

REPLAY prepares the last mission actually played, preserving its board, timer and module count. Press BEGIN REPLAY to arm it. Editing the timer or module count instead prepares a new custom board. Selecting a preset applies that preset's defaults. A board number is shown for identification; this release has no board-number entry control.

Success

Secure every module across every page to win. The timer stops, station lighting turns green, and the vault door lifts to reveal a reward chamber with gold bars and a floating golden relay core. Each win adds one cosmetic core to the locally saved collection, including practice replays. No movement into the vault or manual reward pickup is needed.

GradeRequirement on a completed mission
SNo strikes, with at least half the selected time remaining.
ANo strikes, with less than half the selected time remaining.
BOne strike.
CTwo strikes.

Best completion times are saved locally for each timer/module-count combination. Replays count toward those practice records. The displayed completion streak is for the current app session; a failed mission resets it.

Failure

Zero time or a third strike ends the mission. Unsolved modules lock. A containment cage drops, gas streams downward, and the monitors report OPERATOR DOWN / REMOTE PANEL ACTIVE. These are presentation effects: tracking and the control pad remain available. Choose REPLAY, a new mission, or LOBBY. Returning to the lobby or preparing another mission clears the containment effects.

Sound

In the lobby or briefing, EFFECTS VOLUME changes volume from 0% to 100% in 25% steps. SOUND ON/OFF on the lower-left console edge toggles mute, including during play. Muting does not pause the timer or hide visual clues. Volume and mute state are saved on the headset; turning sound back on from zero restores 75% volume.

The countdown cues become more frequent in the last minute. Use the visible timer for exact time remaining. The Pulse Lock puzzle is read from its lens, not from the countdown sounds.
